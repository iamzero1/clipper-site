Clipper

A private, single-user tool that turns long podcast and interview episodes into captioned vertical clips and uploads them to its owner's own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Downloads and transcribes long-form episodes.
  • Picks clip-worthy moments and renders them as 9:16 captioned videos.
  • Uploads approved clips to the owner's channel and reads their view counts.

Who uses it

Only its owner. Clipper is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and connects to no account other than the owner's own.

Privacy

Clipper accesses the owner's YouTube account solely to upload videos and read statistics for that account. See the privacy policy and terms of service.

Clipper is an internal tool of Mindset Level Up HQ. Clips credit their original creators and link to the full episodes.
