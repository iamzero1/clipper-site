Clipper Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026

Clipper ("the app") is a private, single-user software tool operated by its owner to publish short video clips to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its owner. This policy describes what data the app accesses through Google APIs and how that data is handled.

Data the app accesses

With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, the app uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel and set their title, description, tags and privacy status;
  • read the channel's own video list and view, like and comment counts for videos the app uploaded.

The app does not read the owner's email, contacts, watch history, or any other Google data, and it never accesses any account other than the owner's.

How the data is used

Uploaded videos are clips the owner has created and approved. View counts are stored to measure which clips perform well. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling, or resale.

Where the data is stored

The Google OAuth token and all statistics are stored locally on the owner's own computer in a private database. No data is sent to any server operated by the app's owner or by any third party other than Google's own APIs.

Sharing

The app does not share, sell, or transfer Google user data to anyone.

Limited Use disclosure

Clipper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as the owner keeps the app installed. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which invalidates the stored token immediately. Deleting the app's local data folder removes everything else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the Mindset Level Up HQ YouTube channel or the support email listed on the app's Google consent screen.
